title: "Brand your portal"

---

Your return portal should match your corporate design from day one. Upload a logo and a background image and adjust the colors. This way your customers instantly recognize your brand during the return process.

## Setup

You make all adjustments under **Settings → Branding**.

<Frame><img src="/images/docs/Onboarding/BasicSettings03.png" alt="Uploading logo and background image" /></Frame>

### 1. Upload your logo

Upload your logo as a **.png** file.

- Minimum size: **500 x 500 pixels**.
- Avoid whitespace around the logo where possible.
- Ideally, the logo is rectangular and wider than it is tall. This way it looks best on mobile and desktop.

### 2. Upload a background image

The background image is shown when customers log in to register a return. Use it to personalize your portal, e.g. with your own stock photos.

- Recommended resolution: **1920 x 1080** (Full HD) or **3840 x 2160** (4K).
- Format: **.jpg/JPEG**.

### 3. Adjust colors

In the rest of the return process, you customize the portal mainly through colors:

- **Highlight Color**: Emphasizes small details and important process steps, e.g. the loading animation.
- **Action Color**: Shows interactions through a color change, e.g. when the **Next** button is clicked. We recommend a dark color.

Enter the colors as hex codes. The preview shows the color right away.

## More customization

You can also adjust fonts, individual texts, and the portal languages.
