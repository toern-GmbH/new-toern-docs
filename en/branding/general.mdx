In the **Branding** section, you adapt the return portal to your corporate design. This makes the return process feel like part of your shop for your customers. Among other things, you can upload a logo and a background image and customize colors and fonts.

## Logo

Your logo lets customers recognize your brand in the return process right away. Simply upload a **.png** file.

Keep in mind:

- The logo should be at least **500 x 500 pixels**.
- Avoid whitespace around the logo where possible (see the hints in the settings).
- A logo that is wider than it is tall looks best in the portal. This optimizes the display on mobile and desktop.

## Background Image

In addition to the logo, you can upload a background image. It's shown when customers register their return. This lets you personalize the portal further with your brand's own images.

- Recommended resolution: **1920 x 1080** (Full HD) or **3840 x 2160** (4K).
- Recommended format: **.jpg/JPEG**.

## Highlight and Action Colors

In the rest of the return process, you adapt the portal to your brand mainly through colors and fonts.

You can set two colors:

- **Highlight Color**: Emphasizes small accents and important process steps, for example the loading animation.
- **Action Color**: Indicates interactions through a color change. We recommend a dark color. One example is the **Next** button, which switches to the darker toern green when clicked.

Enter the colors as a **hex code**. The color is shown directly in the preview.

## Font Configuration

You can also customize the fonts in the return portal. There is a **Primary**, **Secondary**, and **Tertiary Font**. Fonts are set via **Google Fonts**. You can also find instructions via the info button in the settings.

### Add a font

1. Open [fonts.google.com](https://fonts.google.com).
2. Select the font family and the styles or weights you want (e.g. 400, 600).
3. In the right panel, click **Get embed code**.
4. Switch to the **@import** tab. You'll see something like this:

   ```css
   @import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;600&display=swap");
   ```

5. Copy only the URL inside `url('...')`, starting with `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2`. Example:

   ```text
   https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400;600&display=swap
   ```

6. Paste the URL into the field for the Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary Font (Google CSS URL).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/docs/Settings/BrandingNew04.png" alt="Font customization" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  You can include several font families by adding more `&family=...` parts.
  Google provides these in the embed code. Keep `display=swap` in the URL for
  better rendering.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  - Don't paste the whole `@import ...;` line, only the HTTPS URL.
  - Don't paste a `<link>` tag. Use the **@import** tab and copy the URL.
  - Don't use `fonts.gstatic.com` links or direct `.ttf` or `.otf` file links.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The Google Fonts URLs are only used for preview and testing. In production,
  fonts are served GDPR-compliantly via our CDN for security reasons.
</Note>

## Limitations

- Logos should be rectangular and wider than tall, as shown in the settings. The height in the portal is limited.
- You can only set fonts via Google Fonts. They are, however, hosted GDPR-compliantly on our servers.
- So far, you can only customize the order number placeholder and an extra info text on the login page. All other info boxes and texts are set by toern.
