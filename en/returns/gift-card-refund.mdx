---
title: "Gift card refund"
description: "Offer your customers a gift card instead of a refund and keep the revenue in your shop."
---

With gift cards, you offer your customers an alternative to a regular refund. This keeps the revenue in your shop instead of paying it back directly. Incentives like a higher gift card value or waiving the return fee make the gift card more attractive.

## How it works

Your customers select the gift card in the second step of the return process. By default, the **original refund** is selected. So your customers have to actively choose the gift card.

This is what the selection looks like in the return portal:

![Selecting the gift card in the return portal](/images/docs/Retourenportal---Standardprozess/Gutscheine01.png)

![Gift card selected](/images/docs/Retourenportal---Standardprozess/Gutschein02.png)

Your customers then confirm the return in a final step. Until then, they can still go back to the [item selection](/en/returns/return-portal) and change the return.

Just like a regular refund, the gift card is usually created automatically as soon as the return's classification is complete:

1. toern creates the gift card in Shopify.
2. Shopify sends an email notification to your customer. You configure its layout in Shopify.
3. The gift card is stored in Shopify. toern links it in the return in Shopify so you can view it if needed.

You can also create a gift card manually during a manual refund. The gift card always applies as the refund method for the entire return, not per item.

<Warning>
  If toern handles refunds, your fulfiller should disable refunds. Otherwise, gift cards can't be issued. Learn more under [Refund](/en/returns/refund#shopify-restock).
</Warning>

## Setup

You activate and configure gift cards under **Settings → Refund**.

### Activate gift cards (Gift Card Refunds)

Use the toggle under **Gift Card Refunds** to activate gift cards in general.

![Gift card settings](/images/docs/Settings/RefundNew07.png)

### Configure gift cards (Gift Card Configuration)

Under **Gift Card Configuration**, you define how attractive your gift cards are and how long they're valid:

- **Bonus percentage**: This percentage is added to the refund amount. It makes the gift card more interesting for your customers compared to cash back.
- **Maximum amount**: If this amount is exceeded, the gift card can't be selected.
- **Expiration**: If the gift card isn't redeemed within this time, it becomes invalid.

![Gift card configuration](/images/docs/Settings/RefundNew08.png)

![Gift card expiration](/images/docs/Settings/RefundSettings03.png)

### Waive the return fee for gift cards (Remove Returnfee for Giftcards)

As an extra incentive, you can waive the return fee when your customers choose a gift card. The gift card amount is then calculated without deducting the return fee.

![Remove return fee for gift cards](/images/docs/Settings/RefundNew09.png)

## Limitations

- Your customers can choose between the original refund and a gift card. Store credit that can be paid out again as a balance isn't possible.
