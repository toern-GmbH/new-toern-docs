In **user management** you create, edit, and delete user accounts for your team. Ideally, add all colleagues who should work with toern before you go live. Typically these are people from customer support, e-commerce, and product management.

You can open user management via **User Management** in the menu or directly via [this link](https://www.admin.re-toern.de/register?page=1&pageSize=10).

![User management overview](/images/docs/Dashboardprozess/Nutzermanagement01.png)

## Roles

These roles are available on brand level:

1. **Brand Admin**: Can view and adjust all settings and has access to all pages and features in the dashboard. The first user, created via the Shopify installation or by toern, is a Brand Admin.
2. **Brand User**: Has no access to the settings. All other pages and features stay accessible. Returns can be managed through the entire process.
3. **Support User**: Responsible for support tasks, mainly managing returns and customer tickets. This role can neither view nor adjust settings.

## Create a new user

1. Open **User Management**.
2. Click **Add New User**.
3. Enter the full **name** and the correct **email address**.
4. Select a **role**. You can also create additional Brand Admins.
5. Click **Create User**.

The new user receives their login credentials by email.

## Edit or delete users

**Edit**: Click the edit icon. You can change a user's **name** and **role**.

**Delete**: Click the trash icon. Confirm in the pop-up with **Delete** that the user should really be deleted.

![Delete users](/images/docs/Dashboardprozess/Nutzermanagement03.png)
