In the **User Settings** you see your profile and set personal preferences: whether the toernbot assistant is shown, whether you get an email when someone tags you, and whether you accept cookies. These settings only apply to your own account. Other users of your brand are not affected, and only you can change them.

## Open the user settings

1. Click your profile at the bottom left of the menu.
2. Open **User Settings**.

Alternatively, open the user settings directly via [this link](https://www.admin.re-toern.de/user-settings).

![Open User Settings](/images/docs/Dashboardprozess/Nutzermanagement04.png)

All changes are saved immediately. There is no save button. After each change, toern briefly confirms that the setting was updated.

## Your profile

The **Your profile** section shows your account details:

- **Name**: your name
- **Email**: the email address you log in with
- **Role**: your role, e.g. Brand Admin, Brand User or Support User

You can't change these details here yourself. A Brand Admin changes name and role in the [user management](/en/dashboard/user-management). Learn more about the roles under [Roles](/en/dashboard/user-management#roles).

## toernbot

The **toernbot Assistant Widget** is an assistant shown in the dashboard that helps you with questions about toern. Use the toggle to show or hide the widget. It is enabled by default.

The setting takes effect immediately on all pages of the dashboard.

## Notifications

### Tag notification email

When a colleague tags you in a note with **@**, you always get a notification in the dashboard. With **Tag notification email** you additionally get an instant email to your account address. This option is disabled by default.

Learn how tagging and notifications work under [Notes and notifications](/en/dashboard/manage-returns#notes-and-notifications).

<Tip>
  Enable the email notification if you don't work in the dashboard all the time but want to react quickly to questions about returns.
</Tip>

## Privacy – GDPR

To keep improving the dashboard, toern uses cookies to track interactions with the platform. Under **Cookie consent** you accept or decline these cookies:

- **Accept All**: You consent to all cookies.
- **Reject All**: You decline all cookies.

Next to **Cookie consent** you see your current status and when you last changed it:

| Status       | Meaning                                  |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Pending**  | You haven't made a decision yet.         |
| **Accepted** | You have consented to all cookies.       |
| **Rejected** | You have declined all cookies.           |

You can change your decision at any time.

<Note>
  As a SaaS platform, we rely on information about how the dashboard is used. That's why we ask you to accept all cookies. We use the tracking information exclusively for internal analysis.
</Note>
