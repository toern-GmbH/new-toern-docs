With **DPD Web Connect**, toern creates your DPD return labels automatically. It's the easiest way to connect DPD. All you need is your Delis ID and your DPD password.

You can find general information about return methods and return countries under [Return methods](/en/returns/return-methods).

## Setup

### Request access to DPD Web Connect

You get your credentials from your DPD depot. Before you receive a Delis ID, your depot must activate your access to the DPD Web Service.

1. Fill in the [DPD Web Services registration form](/images/docs/DPD/RegistrationFormWebServicesDPD.pdf).
2. Send the form to your DPD depot.
3. The depot requests access to DPD Web Connect. Activation usually happens the day after the request.

### Add the access credentials in toern

Enter the following in the [DPD settings](https://www.admin.re-toern.de/settings/return-shipping-service/dpd-webconnect) in toern:

- **Delis ID** (= your DPD username)
- **your DPD password**

Once the data is entered and verified, the connection to DPD is active.

![DPD Web Connect Access Credentials](/images/docs/DPD/DPDNew01.png)

### Country Address Mapping

For toern to create return labels via DPD, you store the return addresses per country in the **DPD Country Address Mapping**.

- Every country that accepts returns needs an entry in the DPD Country Address Mapping.
- Every entry needs the correct address.
- The address can differ per country, but doesn't have to.

![DPD Country Address Mapping](/images/docs/DPD/DPDNew03.png)

![DPD Country Map](/images/docs/DPD/DPD01.png)

## Shipment tracking

For toern to track your DPD returns, it needs the **DPD Push URL** (Tracking Push Service). You request it from your DPD contact. This requires special permissions that DPD has to approve.

1. In the DPD settings in toern, click **Generate DPD URL and Download Form**. You get the required form.
2. Send the form to your DPD contact.
3. As soon as DPD confirms that the push URL is active, toern can track your returns.

![DPD Shipment Tracking](/images/docs/DPD/DPDNew02.png)

Alternatively, download the [Tracking Push Service registration form](/images/docs/DPD/Anmeldeformular_Tracking-Push-Service.pdf), sign in to [DPD Webservices](https://esolutions.dpd.com/entwickler.aspx) and send the completed form to support@re-toern.de. We fill in the technical part and send it back to you. Then you send it to your DPD contact.
