With the GLS connection, toern creates return labels for your customers automatically. You need an active GLS customer contract and a developer app at GLS.

For standard returns, toern uses the API **Shop Returns Customer API v3**.

You can find general information about return methods and return countries under [Return methods](/en/returns/return-methods).

## Setup

### Create a developer account and app

toern needs a developer app with a matching **Key** and **Secret**. GLS IT then connects these credentials to your customer account. You create the app on the [GLS Dev Platform](https://dev-portal.gls-group.net/).

1. Open the [GLS Dev Platform](https://dev-portal.gls-group.net/).
2. Click **Sign In** in the top right.
3. If you don't have an account yet, click **Create an account**.

   ![GLS Sign In](/images/docs/GLS/image.png)

4. Fill in your company information and click **Create Account**.
5. Once logged in, click your profile in the top right and then **Apps**.

   ![GLS Apps](/images/docs/GLS/image-1.png)

6. Click **New App**.

   ![GLS New App](/images/docs/GLS/image-2.png)

7. Give the app a name, e.g. "toern Retourenportal". Activate these APIs with **Enable**:
   - **Authentication API v2**
   - **Shop Returns Customer API v3**
   - **Shop Returns Sandbox API v3** (optional)

   ![GLS app name](/images/docs/GLS/image-3.png)

   ![GLS API permissions](/images/docs/GLS/image-4.png)

8. Click **Save**.

### Add the credentials in toern

You need this information from the app you created:

- **App ID**
- **Key** (Client ID)
- **Secret** (Client secret)

![GLS App ID](/images/docs/GLS/image-5.png)

Enter it in the [GLS settings](https://www.admin.re-toern.de/settings/return-shipping-service/gls) in the toern dashboard. Alternatively, you can send it to us by email at support@re-toern.de.

![GLS Integration](/images/docs/GLS/GLSNew01.png)

### Connect the developer app to your customer account

To activate the app and connect it to your customer account, a GLS IT employee in your region has to link it in the background. This requires your GLS customer number.

We're happy to handle the contact with GLS. We already have the right contacts in the different regions.

1. Enter App ID, Key and Secret in toern.
2. Send your GLS customer number to support@re-toern.de.
3. We take care of the rest.
